# About Bookshelf

Bookshelf is a reading discovery and personal library companion app built by **Huda AbuSharr**.

## What Bookshelf Offers

- Discover books through featured picks, categories, Goodreads-backed highlights, and personalized recommendations
- Search and browse book metadata from Google Books and Open Library
- Open previews, external reading links, downloads, or EPUBs when available
- Read supported EPUBs in the in-app reader with saved progress
- Save, favorite, and mark books as read
- Sync your reading state with your account
- Scan book covers with camera or gallery to identify books quickly
- Get optional AI-powered summaries, scanner help, and recommendations
- Use Magic Discover to find books with themes similar to a movie
- Optional premium purchases to remove ads or unlock AI Premium actions

## Transparency
