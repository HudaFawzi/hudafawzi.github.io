# About Bookshelf

Bookshelf is a reading discovery and personal library companion app built by **Huda AbuSharr**.

## What Bookshelf Offers

- Discover books through featured picks, categories, bundled Goodreads-derived discovery picks, and library-based recommendations
- Search and browse book metadata from Google Books and Open Library
- Open previews, external reading links, downloads, PDFs, or EPUBs when available
- Read supported EPUBs in the in-app reader with saved progress and reader preferences
- Save, favorite, and mark books as read
- Continue as a guest or sign in to sync reading state and library data across devices
- Scan book covers with the camera or gallery to identify books quickly
- Use optional AI-powered chat, summaries, scanner help, and recommendations
- Use Magic Discover to find books with themes, tone, or stories similar to a movie
- Use optional Google Play purchases for Remove Ads or AI Premium actions
- Customize appearance with light/dark theme, dynamic color, download preferences, and optional notifications
- Use Bookshelf in supported languages

## Important to Know

- Full-book access is not guaranteed for every title because previews, EPUBs, downloads, and external reading links depend on third-party providers.
- AI responses are generated automatically and may be inaccurate, so important details should be verified.
- Bundled Goodreads-derived discovery picks do not send your personal data to Goodreads.
- Community reviews, user ratings, and public reader profiles are not included in this release.
- Non-premium users may see ads, including banner, interstitial, native, or rewarded formats.
