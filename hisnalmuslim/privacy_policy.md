# Privacy Policy for Hisn Al Muslim

**Last updated:** August 15, 2026

Hisn Al Muslim respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Android app handles information when you use it.

## Information we collect

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising or analytics.

The following preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can work as expected:

- Favorite adhkar categories
- Theme preference
- App language preference
- Daily reminder preference

This information stays in the app's local storage. If Android device backup is enabled, Android may include app data in a device backup according to your device and Google account settings.

## Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules notifications locally on your device. You can disable reminders at any time in the app's Settings or through your device notification settings. Android controls whether the app can post notifications.

## Contacting us

Selecting **Contact us** opens your email app with our support address filled in. An email is sent only if you choose to send it. The contents of an email you send are handled by your email provider and by us in order to respond to your request.

## Third-party services and external links

The app may use Google Play services to provide its font experience. When you choose to rate the app, Google Play's privacy policy applies. When you share the app, the privacy policy of the app or service you choose from Android's share sheet applies.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The most current version will be available in the app's Settings and in this file.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at [huda.apps.developer@gmail.com](mailto:huda.apps.developer@gmail.com).
